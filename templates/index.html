    FAQ

    Quick answers for store owners

    Do I need technical skills to set up?

    No. The setup is built around simple channel connections and content uploads, so most merchants can get started without coding help.

    Which platforms are supported?

    Sellervai supports WhatsApp, Facebook Messenger, Instagram, and Telegram.

    How does the AI learn about my products?

    You can upload product lists, PDFs, and store details. The AI uses that content to answer customers and recommend relevant items.

    Is my data and my customers' data secure?

    Yes. Tokens are stored securely, and the product is designed to keep merchant data within the merchant's own system.

    Can I try it for free?

    Yes. The Free plan is meant for trying the flow before you move to a paid plan.

    What languages does the AI support?

    The assistant is built for multilingual use, so it can work across common South-Asian and global customer conversations.
